🔒 Locker Lock Practice

Learn to open a combination lock before the first day of school!

How it works

1Turn RIGHT ↻ (clockwise) 3 full times, then stop on the first number.
2Turn LEFT ↺ (counter-clockwise). Go past the second number once, then stop on it the second time.
3Turn RIGHT ↻ straight to the third number. Don't go past it!
4Pull the lock open! 🎉 Made a mistake? Just start over at step 1.

Tips for the first day

  • Go slow on the last two numbers. Speed comes later. Being careful is what matters.
  • Line the number up with the mark at the top. Being off by one usually still works, but more than that won't.
  • Messed up? Don't worry. Spin the dial right a few times and start again. Everyone does this!
  • Pull firmly. Some lockers also need you to lift the handle or latch after the last number.
  • Keep your combination private, and keep a copy somewhere safe at home in case you forget it.
  • Try Challenge mode (turn off "Show hints") until you can open it without help every time.
